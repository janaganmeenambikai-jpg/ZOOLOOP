What We Do

Built for every pet. Trusted by every owner.

Three core capabilities working in concert — so you never miss a signal your pet is trying to send.

01

Health Monitoring

Continuous vitals tracking including heart rate, temperature, and respiratory patterns. Baseline deviations trigger instant notifications to your phone.

02

Activity Intelligence

Step counts, rest cycles, and movement patterns analyzed against breed-specific norms. Know when your pet is under-exercised — or overdoing it.

03

Behavioral Insights

Long-term behavioral data surfaces subtle changes that even attentive owners miss. Patterns that predict stress, illness, or aging — weeks in advance.
