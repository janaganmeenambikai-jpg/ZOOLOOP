ZooLoop is a smart animal-monitoring technology platform designed to make animal care more intelligent, connected, and data-driven. It combines sensors, computer vision, IoT, and analytics to help monitor animal activity, environment, health indicators, and safety.
ZooLoop aims to reduce the challenges involved in continuous animal monitoring. It helps caregivers access meaningful, real-time information and identify unusual conditions or behavioral changes more efficiently.
ZooLoop can be useful for: Zoos and wildlife parks · Animal shelters and rescue organizations · Veterinary and animal-care facilities · Research institutions · Farms and specialized animal facilities · Organizations involved in animal welfare and conservation.
