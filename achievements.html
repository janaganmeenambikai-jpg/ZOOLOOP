Key Recognition

2nd Digital India RISC-V (DIR-V) Hackathon 2025

ZooLoop successfully participated in the National Entrepreneurship Challenge and showcased groundbreaking indigenous solutions for smart pet healthcare monitoring.

The recognition was presented by Prof. V. Kamakoti, Director of IIT Madras, a prominent leader in India’s semiconductor and indigenous computing initiatives, and Shri Rajeev Chandrasekhar, former Union Minister of State for Electronics & Information Technology.
