ZooLoop Smart Pet Monitoring System

Intelligent Wellness Platform

ZooLoop Smart Pet Monitoring System is an intelligent pet monitoring and wellness system designed to provide real-time health insights, activity tracking, behavioral analysis, and proactive pet care assistance through smart sensing technologies and AI-powered analytics.

Engineered with ergonomic lightweight materials, non-invasive bio-sensors, and multi-network connectivity, ZooLoop keeps you seamlessly connected to your pet's well-being 24/7.

Key Capabilities

Real-Time Vitals

Continuous tracking of heart rate variability, body temperature, and respiratory trends with instant anomaly detection.

Activity Intelligence

Precision step metrics, caloric expenditure, and rest-cycle profiling customized for your pet's age and breed.

Behavioral AI

Advanced algorithmic detection of scratching, shaking, restlessness, and stress indicators weeks before symptoms become severe.

GPS & Geofencing

High-accuracy GPS tracking with custom safe zones and instant boundary escape notifications sent to your smartphone.

Vet-Ready Reports

One-click comprehensive clinical summaries and historical graphs ready to export directly to your veterinarian.

Long Battery Life

Ultra-efficient power management delivering up to 14 days of continuous monitoring on a single quick-charge cycle.

System Architecture & Modules

HM — Health Monitoring Module

▼
  • Continuous heart rate & pulse rhythm tracking
  • Skin and ambient temperature sensor arrays
  • Respiratory baseline pattern profiling
  • Clinical vital anomaly thresholds & notifications

AT — Activity Tracking Module

▼
  • 6-axis IMU precision motion sensing
  • Active play vs. rest cycle automated logging
  • Breed-calibrated daily exertion targets
  • Historical activity comparison charts

BA — Behavior Analysis Module

▼
  • On-device machine learning behavior classification
  • Excessive scratching and head shaking alert
  • Sleep disturbance and anxiety recognition
  • Wellness recommendation engine

CC — Cloud & Vet Connectivity

▼
  • End-to-end encrypted cloud synchronization
  • Multi-user family and caretaker sharing
  • Automated monthly PDF vet health export
  • RESTful API integration for veterinary clinics
